PRINCIPLE
Horizontal to vertical privilege escalation

Horizontal privilege escalation means accessing another normal user’s account.
But if the attacker uses this trick on an admin account, it becomes vertical escalation, because they now have more power than they should.
For example, if the attacker can steal or reset an admin's password, they can log in as the admin.
Once they do that, they have full control — that’s vertical privilege escalation.



An attacker logs in with an id as /myaccount?id=321

The attacker changes a URL like:

/myaccount?id=456

to look at someone else’s account.

If that account belongs to an admin, they might see:

The admin’s private data

A way to change the admin's password

Or even admin-only tools and buttons

That means they went from being a normal user to having admin access — a vertical privilege escalation.
